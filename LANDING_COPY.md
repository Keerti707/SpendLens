## FAQ

### 1. Is SpendLens free?

Yes. SpendLens is free to use. The goal is to help teams understand whether their AI spend is reasonable before asking for contact details.

### 2. Do I need to create an account?

No. You can run an audit without logging in.

### 3. Why does SpendLens ask for email after the audit?

Email is requested only after the audit result is shown. This lets users see value first and then request the full report.

### 4. How does SpendLens calculate savings?

SpendLens uses rule-based checks for plan fit, expected seat cost, overlapping tools, and high API spend. The calculations are deterministic instead of AI-generated so they can be tested and explained.

### 5. Does SpendLens expose private company details in shared reports?

No. Public report pages show savings metadata and report information, not private lead details like email, company, or role.

### 6. Is the AI summary used for financial calculations?

No. AI is used only to summarize the already-computed audit results. The actual audit math is rule-based.

### 7. Who is this for?

SpendLens is built for startup founders, engineering managers, and operators who manage AI subscriptions and want a fast second opinion on spend efficiency.
