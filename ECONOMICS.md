# Economics

## Goal

SpendLens is designed as a free lead-generation product for Credex. The tool creates value first by helping startups identify AI spend waste, then captures qualified leads when users request the full audit report.

The business logic is simple:

> If SpendLens helps founders discover meaningful AI spend savings, some of those founders become Credex leads for discounted AI infrastructure credits.

## What a Converted Lead Is Worth

A converted lead for Credex is a startup that completes an audit, has meaningful AI spend, books a consultation, and purchases discounted AI credits.

Because exact Credex margins are private, I used a rough estimate:

- Average high-intent lead monthly AI spend: $2,000
- Potential credit purchase value: $10,000–$25,000
- Estimated gross margin / marketplace spread: 10–20%
- Estimated gross value per converted customer: $1,000–$5,000

For planning, I would use a conservative expected value of:

```text
$2,000 per converted customer.
This is intentionally conservative. If Credex handles larger startup or enterprise credit deals, the value could be much higher.

Funnel Assumptions

A reasonable early funnel could look like:

1,000 visitors
→ 350 audit starts
→ 220 completed audits
→ 80 email captures
→ 20 high-savings leads
→ 6 consultation bookings
→ 2 credit purchases

Using the $2,000 converted-customer value:

2 purchases × $2,000 = $4,000 expected gross value
Conversion Rates Needed

The important conversion points are:

Audit completed → lead captured

Target: 25–40%

This should be achievable because the lead gate appears after value is shown, not before.

Lead captured → consultation booked

Target: 15–30%

This depends heavily on the savings amount. Leads with more than $500/month in savings should be routed more aggressively toward Credex.

Consultation booked → credit purchase

Target: 20–40%

If Credex can prove the discount is real and relevant, high-savings users should have strong buying intent.

CAC by Channel

Because the first GTM plan uses mostly organic channels, CAC is mainly time cost.

Channel	Estimated CAC	Notes
Founder DMs	$0 paid / high time cost	Best for first feedback
Reddit / Indie Hackers	$0 paid / medium time cost	Good for honest user reactions
Product Hunt	$0 paid / medium time cost	Good for launch spike
Founder Twitter/X	$0 paid / high consistency cost	Works if posts show real examples
Credex-owned audience	Lowest CAC	Strongest because audience already matches the problem

Credex’s unfair CAC advantage is that it already has a relevant offer: discounted AI infrastructure credits. SpendLens is not just content marketing; it is a diagnostic tool that qualifies the user.

What Makes This Profitable

Assume:

Monthly visitors: 10,000
Audit completion rate: 25%
Lead capture rate after audit: 30%
High-savings lead rate: 20%
Consultation booking rate: 20%
Purchase conversion rate: 25%
Value per converted customer: $2,000

Math:

10,000 visitors
× 25% audit completion = 2,500 completed audits

2,500 completed audits
× 30% lead capture = 750 captured leads

750 leads
× 20% high-savings = 150 high-savings leads

150 high-savings leads
× 20% consultation booking = 30 consultations

30 consultations
× 25% purchase conversion = 7.5 purchases

7.5 purchases
× $2,000 value = $15,000/month expected gross value

This would make the tool valuable even before considering brand awareness, retargeting, and repeat usage.

Path to $1M ARR Impact

For SpendLens to drive $1M ARR-equivalent impact in 18 months, at least one of these must be true:

Credex average converted-customer value is higher than $2,000.
The tool reaches large teams with much higher AI spend.
The audit becomes a strong recurring benchmark that users revisit quarterly.
The tool becomes embedded in founder/operator communities.
Credex uses the audit as the default qualification step for inbound leads.

A possible path:

50 converted customers/month
× $2,000 expected value
= $100,000/month
= $1.2M/year

This requires either strong organic distribution or a much higher average deal value.

Key Economic Risk

The biggest risk is low trust. If the recommendations feel vague or inflated, users will not submit their email or book a consultation.

That is why SpendLens keeps the audit math rule-based and explains recommendations in plain language. The product should be honest when savings are low instead of manufacturing fake urgency.
```
